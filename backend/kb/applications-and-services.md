# Applications and online services citizens can use

## Report a suspicious call, SMS or WhatsApp number on Chakshu
Chakshu is a service on the Sanchar Saathi portal of the Department of Telecommunications. Use it to report suspected fraud communication such as a fake KYC call, a threatening call or a phishing SMS or WhatsApp message, even if you did not lose money. Open sancharsaathi.gov.in, choose Chakshu report suspected fraud communication, enter the fraudster's number or message details, the date and a short description, add a screenshot if you have one and submit. The link is on this portal under Report Fake Call or SMS.

## Block a lost or stolen mobile phone
Use the Sanchar Saathi portal (sancharsaathi.gov.in) option to block a lost or stolen mobile. Keep your phone's IMEI number (dial star hash 06 hash on a working phone or check the box or bill), the mobile number, and a copy of the police complaint or FIR for the lost phone. After the request, the phone is blocked across mobile networks in India. If the phone is found, you can unblock it from the same portal using the request ID. Also tell your bank to block UPI and cards linked to that number.

## Check how many mobile connections are in your name
On the Sanchar Saathi portal use the option to know the mobile connections registered in your name. If you find numbers you do not use or never took, report them from the same page so they can be verified and disconnected. This helps prevent SIM based fraud.

## Report on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
Complaints for financial fraud, social media crime, hacking, online harassment and other cyber crimes are filed on cybercrime.gov.in. Crimes related to women and children can also be reported anonymously. You can track your complaint status by logging in with the registered mobile number. Quote the acknowledgement number whenever you contact police or the bank.

## Complaint letter application to the Station House Officer
A written application to the SHO of the nearest police station requesting registration of an FIR and investigation. It should have your details, the date and description of the incident, the amount lost, the fraudster details, enclosures and your signature. This portal can draft it in English or Hindi; the assistant will ask you for the details one by one. Print it, sign it, submit it at the station and keep an acknowledged copy.

## Application to your bank for an unauthorised transaction dispute
A written complaint to the bank branch or the nodal officer asking to reverse an unauthorised transaction. Mention account number, card or UPI details, the transaction ID, date, time and amount, the date and time you reported it by phone and the complaint reference number. Attach the bank statement and the police complaint or 1930 and portal acknowledgement number. If the bank does not resolve it in the time allowed, you can escalate to the bank's grievance officer and then to the RBI Banking Ombudsman through the RBI complaint management portal (cms.rbi.org.in).

## Tenant and domestic servant verification application
Landlords and employers should get tenants and household help verified by the police for safety. The Rajasthan Police citizen services (police.rajasthan.gov.in and the RajCop app) provide tenant and servant verification. You will need the owner's details and property address, the tenant or servant's full name, permanent address, phone number, a copy of Aadhaar or Voter ID, two passport size photographs and the previous employer's or landlord's address and phone number. Submit online or at your local police station and keep the receipt. Confirm the current process with your local station.

## Report intimate image abuse
If someone shares or threatens to share intimate images, take screenshots as evidence, report the content on the platform, use StopNCII.org to help block the images on participating platforms, and file a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. You can report anonymously in the women and children related section. Call 181 for the Women Helpline, or 112 in an emergency.

## Report a cyber incident for an organisation or website
Businesses, institutions and website owners should report hacking, ransomware, data breaches and defacement to CERT-In at incident@cert-in.org.in and to the police. Preserve logs, disconnect affected systems and do not pay the ransom without advice.
