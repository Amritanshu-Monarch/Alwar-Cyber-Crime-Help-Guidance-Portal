# Police Station Reporting and Legal Remedies

## What is Zero FIR
- Under Section 173(1) of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023, an FIR can be registered at ANY police station irrespective of jurisdictional boundaries if a cognizable cyber crime occurred.
- The receiving police station registers a "Zero FIR", issues an acknowledgement to the victim, and transfers the case to the appropriate jurisdictional police station or Cyber Cell.
- No police officer can refuse to register an FIR on the grounds of lack of territorial jurisdiction.

## If Local Police Refuse to Register Your Complaint
1. Note the name, designation, and station of the officer refusing your complaint.
2. Under Section 173(4) of BNSS 2023, send the written complaint by Registered Post or in person to the Superintendent of Police (SP Office, Alwar).
3. In Alwar, approach the Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306 or SP Office at 0144-2337456.
4. You may also lodge an online complaint via Rajasthan Police Portal (police.rajasthan.gov.in) or RajSampark (181).
