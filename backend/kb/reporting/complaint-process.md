# Cyber Crime Complaint Process and Workflow

## End-to-End Filing Sequence
1. Step 1 (Immediate): Call National Helpline 1930 for financial freeze, or 112 for physical emergency.
2. Step 2 (Evidence Preparation): Compile bank statement (with UTR), chat screenshots, fraudster phone numbers, and identity proof.
3. Step 3 (Portal Filing): Lodge complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. Quote the 1930 acknowledgement number.
4. Step 4 (Physical Police Filing): Submit a formal written complaint addressed to the Station House Officer (SHO) of your local police station or Cyber Crime Police Station (CCPS).
5. Always demand a stamped receipt / Diary Number (Roznamcha entry) as proof of submission.
