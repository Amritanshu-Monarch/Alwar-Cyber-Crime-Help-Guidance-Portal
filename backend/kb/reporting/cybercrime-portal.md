# National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in)

## Registration on cybercrime.gov.in
- The official government portal for registering all cyber crime complaints across India: cybercrime.gov.in.
- For crimes against women and children (deepfakes, revenge porn, child abuse, cyber stalking), you can report anonymously or track as an authenticated citizen.
- For financial crimes, you can register using your mobile number and quote the 1930 acknowledgement number.

## Mandatory Information to Enter
1. Incident Category and Sub-category.
2. Date, time, and incident narrative.
3. Suspect details (phone number, website URL, social media handle, bank account/UPI ID).
4. Evidence attachments: PDF/JPEG format of bank statement, screenshots, chat backups.
- After submission, a 14-digit Complaint ID is generated for online tracking.
