# Digital Evidence and Documents for Cyber Crime Complaint

## Documents and Evidence to Keep Ready
When filing any cyber crime complaint at the police station or on cybercrime.gov.in, keep ready:
- Valid Government ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License).
- Full bank account statement or debit SMS showing unauthorized transactions, debit timestamp, and 12-digit UTR/reference number.
- Detailed screenshots of fraudulent messages, WhatsApp/Telegram chats, payment QR codes, and scammer profile pages.
- Phone numbers, email addresses, and bank accounts/UPI IDs of the scammer.
- Copy of complaint acknowledgement receipt from National Helpline 1930.

## Golden Rules for Preserving Evidence
- NEVER delete WhatsApp chats, Telegram conversations, SMS alerts, or phone call logs. Deleting messages destroys crucial electronic evidence required under Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023.
- Take complete screenshots showing the caller ID/phone number, contact name, date, time, and full chat bubble.
- In deepfake, extortion, or blackmail cases, export the full chat backup, preserve the raw video or image file on a pen drive without re-encoding, and record screen captures of threatening video calls if possible.
- If a fraud APK was downloaded, do NOT immediately factory reset the phone; note down the exact APK name and package details for forensic investigation.
