# Digital Arrest and Coerced Bank Transfers

## What is Digital Arrest Scam
- Victims receive calls claiming to be from Mumbai Police, CBI, ED, Customs, or Telecom Department.
- Scammers claim your Aadhaar or mobile number was used for money laundering, drugs, or illegal parcel.
- They put victims under "digital arrest" via Skype or WhatsApp video call, showing fake police stations, uniforms, and fake court arrest warrants.
- They coerce victims into transferring all savings to a "RBI secret verification account" or "government audit account".

## Official Truth and Safety Protocol
- Rule: There is NO provision called "Digital Arrest" under Indian law (BNSS, BNS, or IT Act).
- Police, CBI, ED, Customs, and judges NEVER interrogate citizens, demand money, or place anyone under arrest via WhatsApp or Skype video calls.
- Never transfer money to any account for "verification" or "clearing your name".
- If you transferred money under threat, dial 1930 immediately and contact Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
