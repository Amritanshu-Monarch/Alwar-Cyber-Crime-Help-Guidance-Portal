# OTP Phishing and Impersonation

## Fraudulent OTP Requests
- Callers posing as bank officials, electricity department, courier agents, or telecom representatives demanding OTP for KYC update or bill clearance.
- Rule: No bank, police officer, or government organization ever asks for an OTP or PIN over phone, SMS, or WhatsApp.
- If you shared an OTP and money was deducted:
  1. Immediately call your bank and block net banking and linked cards.
  2. Call 1930 to trigger account freeze across banking rails.
  3. Change all banking login passwords and UPI PINs immediately.
  4. Preserve the SMS containing the OTP and caller number as evidence.
