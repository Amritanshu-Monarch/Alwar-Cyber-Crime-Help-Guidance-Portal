# Online Shopping and Fake Website Fraud

## Fake E-Commerce Stores
- Fraudulent Instagram/Facebook ads offering heavy discounts (e.g. smartphones or branded clothes at 80% off).
- Payment is taken upfront via UPI or QR code, but goods are never delivered.
- Fake courier delivery messages asking for Rs 5 or Rs 10 to update address, leading to account drain.

## Action Plan
- Call 1930 to dispute the UPI transaction.
- Take screenshots of the website URL, advert, invoice, and payment page.
- Report the fake website to cybercrime.gov.in.
