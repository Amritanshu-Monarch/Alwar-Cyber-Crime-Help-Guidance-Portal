# Fake Investment and Trading Scams

## Modus Operandi
- Victims are added to WhatsApp or Telegram groups offering 200% to 500% returns on stocks, IPOs, or crypto.
- Fraudsters share fake trading websites or apps showing inflated profits.
- When the victim attempts to withdraw funds, scammers demand extra money for "tax clearance", "handling charges", or "anti-money laundering fees".
- The funds are never returned and the victim is blocked.

## Reporting and Evidence
- Call 1930 immediately to freeze bank accounts to which you transferred deposits.
- Preserve all chat logs, group member lists, admin phone numbers, deposit receipts, and fake trading app APKs/websites.
- File complaint on cybercrime.gov.in and submit copy to Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
