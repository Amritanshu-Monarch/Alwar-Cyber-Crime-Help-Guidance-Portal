# Credit and Debit Card Fraud

## Unauthorized Card Charges and ATM Clones
- Card skimming at ATMs or POS machines to copy magnetic strip details and clone cards.
- International online transactions where OTP is bypassed because the merchant website is outside India.
- Compromised card details through phishing links or data breaches.

## Action Plan
1. Block the card immediately through mobile banking or bank customer care helpline.
2. Turn off international and contactless transactions via your bank app.
3. Call 1930 to report the unauthorized debit.
4. Notify your bank within 3 working days under RBI guidelines to ensure zero customer liability.
5. In Alwar, report to Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306 or file a written complaint at your local police station.
