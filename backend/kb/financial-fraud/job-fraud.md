# Part-Time Job and Task Scams

## Work from Home, Part-Time Job, and Task Scam Deposits
- Offers received via WhatsApp, SMS, or Telegram to earn Rs 2000 to Rs 5000 per day by liking YouTube videos, rating Google hotels, or writing reviews.
- Victims are paid a small incentive initially (Rs 150 to Rs 500) to build trust.
- Later, scammers demand money deposits for "prepaid tasks", "VIP tasks", "cryptocurrency trading tasks", or "security deposits".
- All deposited money is stolen and accounts are frozen by scammers.

## Action Plan for Job Scams
- Call 1930 immediately to freeze beneficiary bank accounts where deposits were sent.
- Keep transaction IDs (UTR) for every payment made.
- Do not delete Telegram chats, WhatsApp numbers, or task links.
- Report the scam on cybercrime.gov.in and to Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
