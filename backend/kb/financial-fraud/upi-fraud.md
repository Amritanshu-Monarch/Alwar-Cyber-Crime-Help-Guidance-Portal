# UPI Payment Fraud

## How UPI Fraud Happens
- Fraudsters send fake "Collect Requests" or QR codes claiming you will receive money, lottery prizes, or cashback.
- Rule: Entering your UPI PIN always debits money from your account. UPI PIN is NEVER needed to receive money.
- Fraudsters claim to be buyers on OLX/marketplace asking you to scan a QR code to receive advance payment.
- Fake customer support numbers on Google search asking you to make a nominal Re 1 or Rs 5 transfer to verify your account.

## Immediate Steps for UPI Fraud
1. Immediately dial 1930 to register the financial dispute and freeze beneficiary accounts.
2. Open your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and report the transaction as fraud.
3. Call your bank immediately and raise an unauthorized electronic transaction dispute.
4. Keep the 12-digit UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) number ready for police reporting.
5. In Alwar, notify the Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
