# Malicious APKs and Remote Access Malware

## Fake Utility Bills, E-Challan APKs, and Remote Control
- Fraudsters send APK files via WhatsApp or SMS claiming to be electricity bill updates, traffic e-challan, PM Yojana, or courier tracking.
- Once installed, the APK grants SMS reading permissions, accessibility services, and steals OTPs and bank credentials silently.
- Scammers guide victims to install remote desktop apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer QuickSupport, or RustDesk, then view their banking screen and steal credentials.

## Emergency Defense Steps
1. Put the phone in Airplane Mode immediately and turn off Wi-Fi.
2. If AnyDesk or TeamViewer was installed, uninstall it immediately.
3. Uninstall any unknown or recently installed APK files from Settings > Apps.
4. From another clean device, log in to your bank and change net banking password and UPI PIN.
5. If money was deducted, call 1930 immediately and notify Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
