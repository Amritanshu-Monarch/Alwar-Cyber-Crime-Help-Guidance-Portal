# WhatsApp Account Hijacking and Call Forwarding

## Modus Operandi
- Fraudsters call claiming to be courier/telecom and trick victims into dialling code `*21*<number>#` or `*401*<number>#`, which forwards all calls to the fraudster.
- Fraudsters request a WhatsApp registration code via phone call verification and hijack the account.
- Scammers ask contacts for urgent money transfer or send malicious links.

## Recovery Steps
1. Re-register WhatsApp with your phone number and enter the 6-digit SMS verification code. This immediately logs out the hacker.
2. Dial `##21#` or `##002#` on your mobile dialer to cancel any active call forwarding.
3. Enable Two-Step Verification (PIN) inside WhatsApp (Settings > Account > Two-Step Verification).
4. Check WhatsApp Web / Linked Devices and log out of all unknown sessions.
