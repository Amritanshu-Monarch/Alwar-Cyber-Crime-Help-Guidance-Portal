# Phishing Links and Email Hacking

## Identifying Phishing Attempts
- Fake emails or SMS claiming your bank account or PAN card is suspended unless updated via a link.
- Inspect URLs carefully: official Indian government portals end with `.gov.in` or `.nic.in`. Banks never use shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) for password updates.
- If an email account was hacked, use account recovery options, check mailbox forwarding rules (hackers often secretly forward your emails), and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
