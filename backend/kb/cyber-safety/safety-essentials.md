# Cyber Safety Essentials and Best Practices

## Password Hygiene and Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Create strong passwords of at least 12 characters combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never use your date of birth, name, or vehicle number.
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all email, banking, and social media accounts. Prefer authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) over SMS OTP.
- Never share OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, or passwords with anyone, including bank staff or police.

## Protecting Children and Privacy
- Monitor children's online access and educate them never to accept video calls or share private images with strangers.
- Never click on unverified links received via SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram.
- Regularly inspect app permissions on your smartphone and revoke camera, microphone, contacts, and SMS permissions from unneeded apps.
