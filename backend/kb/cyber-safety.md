# Staying safe online

## Rules to protect your money
Never share OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or passwords with anyone, including callers who say they are from the bank, police or a delivery company. You never need to enter a PIN to receive money. Do not scan unknown QR codes or approve unknown collect requests. Do not install apps from links sent on WhatsApp or SMS, especially APK files. Use only the official bank app and website. Set transaction limits on UPI and cards and turn on SMS and app alerts.

## Strong passwords and two factor authentication
Use a long unique password for each important account such as email, banking and social media. A password manager helps. Turn on two factor authentication using an authenticator app or the platform's security option wherever possible. Protect your email account most, because it can reset every other password. Never reuse your banking password anywhere else.

## Spotting phishing links, messages and calls
Warning signs are urgency or threats, requests for OTP or personal details, shortened or misspelled links, unknown senders claiming to be an authority and offers that are too good to be true. Do not click; open the official app or type the website address yourself. Verify by calling the official number printed on your card or passbook. Report suspicious numbers on Chakshu at sancharsaathi.gov.in.

## Keep your phone and computer secure
Install updates, use a screen lock, download apps only from the official Play Store or App Store and check app permissions. Do not give access to contacts, photos or SMS to loan or unknown apps. Do not root or jailbreak your phone. Keep antivirus on computers. Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking.

## Safe use of social media
Keep profiles private, do not accept friend requests from strangers, do not share OTPs, personal documents, address or live location, and be careful with video calls from unknown people. Do not send intimate photos or videos. If someone blackmails you, do not pay; save evidence and report at cybercrime.gov.in.

## Safety for children, teenagers and elders online
Talk openly with children about online strangers, gaming chats and sharing photos, use parental controls, and tell them to inform you if anyone makes them uncomfortable. Child related cyber crime can be reported on cybercrime.gov.in and by calling Childline 1098. Teach elders the golden rules: no OTP sharing, no remote access apps and no money transfer on a phone call; keep 1930 and 8764874306 saved.

## Safe online shopping and payments
Shop on trusted websites with a secure address starting with https, avoid deals asking for advance payment through personal accounts, check seller reviews and prefer cash on delivery or card payment with protection for unknown sellers. Do not pay through links sent in chats. Keep order screenshots.

## Signs that your account or phone may be compromised
Unknown transactions or login alerts, unexpected OTPs, apps you did not install, battery or data draining, friends receiving strange messages from you. Change passwords from a safe device, remove unknown apps, log out of all sessions, inform your bank, and report to 1930 if money is involved.
