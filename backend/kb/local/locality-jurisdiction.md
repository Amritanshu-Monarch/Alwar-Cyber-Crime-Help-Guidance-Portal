# Locality to Police Station Jurisdiction in Alwar

## Locality Mapping Table
- **Kala Kuan, Surya Nagar, Housing Board, Scheme 1, 2, 8** -> Covered by **Aravali Vihar Police Station**.
- **Hope Circus, Purana Katla, Munshi Bagh, Church Road, City Center** -> Covered by **Kotwali Police Station**.
- **Naya Bass, Hasan Khan Mewati Nagar, Transport Nagar, Ambedkar Nagar** -> Covered by **NEB Police Station**.
- **Shivaji Park, Malviya Nagar, Scheme 10** -> Covered by **Shivaji Park Police Station**.
- **Matsya Industrial Area (MIA), RIICO Area** -> Covered by **Udyog Nagar Police Station**.
- **Daudpur, Alwar Rural outskirts** -> Covered by **Sadar Police Station, Alwar**.
- **Women harassment / stalking cases anywhere in Alwar** -> Can approach **Mahila Thana Alwar** directly.
- **Tehsils and Towns**: Bhiwadi -> Phoolbagh Police Station; Tijara -> Tijara Police Station; Behror -> Behror Police Station; Neemrana -> Neemrana Police Station.

If you are unsure of your exact jurisdiction in Alwar, call the Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306 or visit the nearest police station for a Zero FIR.
