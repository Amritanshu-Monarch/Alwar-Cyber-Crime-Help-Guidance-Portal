# Alwar Cyber Crime Unit and Control Room

## Alwar Cyber Crime Police Station (CCPS)
- Specialized cyber crime station located inside the Superintendent of Police (SP) Office Premises, Alwar, Rajasthan.
- Focuses on complex online financial fraud syndicates, technical digital forensics, major hacking cases, and organized cyber extortion.
- Official Email: ccps.alwar@rajpolice.gov.in
- Phone / 24x7 WhatsApp Desk: 8764874306
- SP Office Alwar landline: 0144-2337456

## When to Approach the Cyber Thana vs Local Thana
- Minor disputes, lost phones, or local physical threats can be reported to your local territorial Thana (Kotwali, NEB, Aravali Vihar, Shivaji Park, etc.).
- Complicated technical cases, cryptocurrency frauds, multi-state cyber syndicates, or severe digital extortion cases are investigated by the Cyber Crime Police Station (CCPS).
- Zero FIR can be filed at any local Thana and forwarded to the Cyber Thana.
