# Alwar District Police Stations

## Major Urban Police Stations in Alwar
1. **Kotwali Police Station**: Hope Circus, Purana Katla, Munshi Bagh, Church Road, City Center, Alwar City.
2. **NEB Police Station**: Naya Bass, Hasan Khan Mewati Nagar, Ambedkar Nagar, Transport Nagar, Delhi Gate.
3. **Aravali Vihar Police Station**: Kala Kuan, Housing Board, Surya Nagar, Scheme No. 1, Scheme No. 2, Scheme No. 8.
4. **Shivaji Park Police Station**: Shivaji Park locality, Malviya Nagar, Scheme No. 10, Shanti Kunj.
5. **Mahila Thana Alwar**: Specialized district-wide police station for women's safety, domestic violence, and female cyber harassment complaints.
6. **Sadar Police Station, Alwar**: Alwar Rural areas, Daudpur, Jaipur Road, Delhi Road bypass.
7. **Udyog Nagar Police Station**: Matsya Industrial Area (MIA), RIICO industrial zone.

## Key Sub-Divisional Police Stations in Alwar District
- Bhiwadi (Phoolbagh Police Station), Tijara, Kishangarh Bas, Behror, Neemrana, Shahjahanpur, Ramgarh, Laxmangarh, Rajgarh, Thanagazi.
