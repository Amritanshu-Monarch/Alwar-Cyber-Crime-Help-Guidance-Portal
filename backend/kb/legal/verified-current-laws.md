# Verified Current Cyber Laws and Statutory Rights

## Information Technology Act, 2000 (Amended)
- **Section 43 & 66**: Unauthorized computer access, hacking, data theft, and virus transmission (imprisonment up to 3 years and fine).
- **Section 66C**: Identity theft (fraudulent use of electronic signature, password, or unique identification feature).
- **Section 66D**: Cheating by personation using computer resource (punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to 1 lakh rupees).
- **Section 66E**: Violation of bodily privacy (capturing, publishing, or transmitting images of private area without consent).
- **Section 67 & 67A**: Publishing or transmitting obscene material (67) or sexually explicit/pornographic acts (67A) in electronic form (non-bailable, up to 5 years imprisonment on first conviction).

## Criminal Law (BNS & BNSS 2023)
- **Section 318(4) Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023**: Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property (replaces IPC 420).
- **Section 173 Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023**: Mandatory registration of FIR / Zero FIR for cognizable crimes irrespective of territorial jurisdiction.

## RBI Customer Protection Guidelines (Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18)
- **Zero Customer Liability**: Applicable when unauthorized electronic banking transaction occurs due to contributory fraud/negligence/deficiency on the part of the bank, OR where a third-party breach occurs and the customer notifies the bank within **3 working days** of receiving the transaction alert.
- **Limited Customer Liability**: If notified within 4 to 7 working days, customer liability is limited to transaction value or Rs 5,000 to Rs 10,000 (depending on account type).
- **Customer Negligence**: If customer willingly shared OTP or PIN, the customer bears the liability until the unauthorized transaction is reported to the bank; any debit occurring after reporting is borne by the bank.
