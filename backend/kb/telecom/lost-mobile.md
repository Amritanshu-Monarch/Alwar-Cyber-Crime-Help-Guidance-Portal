# Lost or Stolen Mobile Phone Blocking (CEIR)

## Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR)
- The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) operates the CEIR portal on Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in) to block lost or stolen mobile phones across India.
- Once blocked on CEIR, the phone cannot be used with ANY SIM card across any telecom operator in India.

## Step-by-Step Process
1. Call your telecom provider and block the lost SIM card; obtain a duplicate SIM.
2. File a lost mobile report with Rajasthan Police (online through Rajasthan Police Citizen Portal or at the local police station) and get a digital receipt / LDR number.
3. Visit sancharsaathi.gov.in -> Select "Block Stolen/Lost Mobile (CEIR)".
4. Enter mobile number, 15-digit IMEI 1 & IMEI 2 (found on invoice or box), brand, model, purchase invoice, and police complaint receipt.
5. Note the Request ID. When the phone is found by police, use the same portal to Unblock it.
