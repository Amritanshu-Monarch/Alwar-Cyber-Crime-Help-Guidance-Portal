# SIM Swap Fraud

## Sudden Network Loss and OTP Interception
- Your mobile suddenly shows "No Service" or "Emergency Calls Only" in an area with good network coverage.
- Fraudsters use forged ID documents to request a duplicate SIM from your telecom provider (Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL).
- Once the duplicate SIM activates, your physical SIM is deactivated, and all your banking SMS and OTPs go to the fraudster.

## Immediate Action
1. Contact your telecom operator customer care from another phone immediately to confirm if a duplicate SIM was issued, and block it.
2. Call your bank immediately and freeze net banking and UPI to prevent unauthorized withdrawals.
3. If money has been debited, call 1930 immediately.
4. Visit your telecom operator store with original Aadhaar card to reclaim your SIM.
