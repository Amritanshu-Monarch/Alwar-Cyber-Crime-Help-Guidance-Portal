# Reporting Suspicious Calls, SMS, and Checking Registered SIMs (Chakshu & TAFCOP)

## Chakshu Portal (Suspected Fraud Communication)
- Chakshu is an initiative under Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in) to report suspected fraud communications received via Call, SMS, or WhatsApp BEFORE any financial loss occurs.
- Use Chakshu for fake KYC alerts, fake lottery messages, electric bill threats, sextortion threats, and impersonation attempts.
- Do NOT use Chakshu if financial loss has already happened; for financial loss, call 1930 directly.

## TAFCOP Portal (Check SIM Connections in Your Name)
- TAFCOP (Telecom Analytics for Fraud management and Consumer Protection) allows citizens to check all mobile connections registered against their Aadhaar ID.
- Visit sancharsaathi.gov.in -> Select "Know Your Mobile Connections (TAFCOP)".
- Enter your mobile number, verify OTP. If any unknown number is listed under your name, select "Not My Number" and request de-linking.
