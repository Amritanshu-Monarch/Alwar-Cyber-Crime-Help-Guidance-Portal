# How to report a cyber crime in India

## First 10 minutes after online financial fraud
Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 immediately. This is the Golden Hour: the sooner you report, the better the chance that the money can be frozen before the fraudster moves it. Keep the transaction ID (UTR), the amount, the date and time, your bank or UPI app name and the fraudster's account number, UPI ID or phone number ready. You will receive an acknowledgement number from 1930. Also call the Alwar Cyber Control Room on 8764874306.

## Step by step: register a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
The national portal is cybercrime.gov.in (NCRP, run by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre under the Ministry of Home Affairs). Typical steps: open cybercrime.gov.in and choose to report a cyber crime; choose the category (financial fraud or other cyber crime; there is a separate section for crimes related to women and children where you can also report anonymously); register or log in with your mobile number using an OTP; enter the incident details (what happened, when, where, how); enter suspect details if known (phone numbers, email, social media profile link, website, bank account or UPI ID); upload evidence (screenshots, bank statement, chat export, photos); preview and submit. You receive a complaint acknowledgement number by SMS or email. Use the same login later to track the status. If you called 1930 first, quote that acknowledgement number in the portal. The portal layout can change, so follow the on-screen instructions.

## Tell your bank immediately
Call your bank's 24x7 customer care or use the app to block the card, UPI or net banking, and raise a dispute for the unauthorised transaction. Follow up with a written complaint or email to the bank branch and keep the copy. Under the RBI customer protection rules, if an unauthorised electronic transaction is caused by a third-party breach and you inform the bank within three working days, your liability is generally zero; if you report after that, liability may be limited or depend on the bank's board-approved policy. Report as early as possible and ask the bank for the complaint reference number.

## Complaint at the police station and Zero FIR
You can also give a written complaint at your nearest police station or the cyber cell. Alwar citizens can also contact the Alwar Cyber Control Room on 8764874306. Under the new criminal laws (BNSS) a Zero FIR can be registered at any police station irrespective of where the crime happened and is then transferred to the correct station. Electronic complaints can be accepted by police but are usually required to be signed by the complainant within a few days, so keep your phone reachable and visit the station when asked. Always ask for a copy of the complaint or FIR and note the receipt number.

## What happens after you report
Your complaint is assigned a reference number. For financial fraud, the details are shared with the bank or wallet where the money went so that the amount can be put on hold if it is still there. Police investigate, may call you for a statement and ask for original evidence. Do not delete any messages, chats, apps or call logs. Refunds are not guaranteed and depend on how quickly you reported and on whether the money could be frozen. Never pay anyone who claims they can recover your money for a fee.

## Do not delete evidence
Do not delete any APK file, suspicious app, WhatsApp chat, SMS, email, screenshot, call log or social media message after a fraud. They are digital evidence. Take screenshots of chats, profiles, payment pages and websites before they disappear. Do not uninstall the suspicious app until police advise you. Keep your SIM and phone safe.

## Which helpline for which problem
Cyber financial fraud: 1930. Alwar Cyber Control Room: 8764874306. Police emergency or danger to life: 112. Child in distress: Childline 1098. Women helpline: 181. Aadhaar misuse: UIDAI helpline 1947. Suspicious call, SMS or WhatsApp number: report on Chakshu at sancharsaathi.gov.in. Organisation or website hacked, ransomware or data breach: report to CERT-In at incident@cert-in.org.in.
