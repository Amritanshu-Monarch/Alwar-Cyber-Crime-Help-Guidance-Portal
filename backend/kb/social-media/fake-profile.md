# Fake Profiles on Social Media

## Impersonating Profiles
- Scammers create accounts using your name, photos, and bio on Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn.
- They send friend requests to your contacts asking for urgent money, emergency hospital assistance, or sending inappropriate messages.

## Action Plan
1. Copy the exact URL / link of the fake profile.
2. Tell your family and friends to report the account as "Pretending to be someone I know".
3. Submit an impersonation report to the platform (Meta / Instagram Help Center).
4. Take full screenshots of the fake profile, bio, posts, and message requests.
5. Report to cybercrime.gov.in and submit complaint to Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
