# Cyber Harassment, Abusive Messages, and Cyber Stalking

## Online Harassment and Stalking
- Continuous abusive messages, phone calls, threats, or unwanted advances across WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, or email.
- Monitoring online activity, commenting maliciously, or doxxing (publishing victim's personal phone number or address).

## Evidence and Legal Redress
- Never engage in counter-abuse; preserve every message, call recording, and screenshot with timestamps.
- Women victims can reach out directly to Mahila Thana Alwar or call Women Helpline 1090.
- Cyber stalking is punishable under Indian law (Section 78 of BNS 2023 / Section 354D IPC and Section 66E of IT Act).
- File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and report to Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
