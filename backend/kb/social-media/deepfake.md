# Deepfake and AI Morphed Media Crimes

## What is Deepfake Crime
- Using generative AI, face-swap tools, or voice cloning to superimpose a victim's face or voice onto obscene, compromising, or fraudulent videos/audio.
- Deepfakes are used for blackmail, extortion, sexual harassment, character assassination, or financial deception.

## Immediate Digital Evidence Preservation (DO NOT DELETE)
- Do NOT delete the video, audio, or image sent by the perpetrator. It is prime digital forensic evidence.
- Save the original media files with their file metadata (EXIF data, timestamp, file size) onto an external USB drive or secure folder.
- Take complete screenshots of the chat messages, sender profile, contact number, and timestamp of receipt.
- Copy the exact profile URL, channel URL, or post URL where the deepfake was uploaded or shared.

## How to Report
- Report immediately to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in under "Report Crime Related to Women/Children" or "Report Other Cyber Crimes".
- To prevent dissemination of non-consensual intimate images, file a stop-request on stopncii.org.
- File a formal written complaint with Alwar Police. For women victims in Alwar, visit Mahila Thana Alwar or the Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306.
- Under Section 66E (privacy violation) and 67/67A (obscene/sexually explicit content) of the Information Technology Act 2000, creating or transmitting sexually explicit deepfakes is a punishable non-bailable offense.
