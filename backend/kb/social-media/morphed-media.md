# Morphed Photographs and Image Manipulation

## Photo Morphing Harassment
- Perpetrators take normal photos from public social media profiles (Instagram, Facebook) and morph them with vulgar or nude imagery to threaten or shame victims.
- Often done by rogue instant loan apps or cyber blackmailers threatening to circulate images to friends, family, and phone contacts.

## Crucial Steps
1. Do NOT pay any ransom or succumb to demands. Paying money leads to repeated extortion demands.
2. Preserve original messages, threat audios, and phone numbers.
3. Report the offending accounts directly on the social media platform (Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp) for harassment and impersonation.
4. Report on cybercrime.gov.in and submit physical complaint with digital evidence to Alwar Cyber Control Room (8764874306) or nearest police station.
