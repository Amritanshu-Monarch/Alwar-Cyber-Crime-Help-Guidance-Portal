# Sextortion and Video Call Blackmail

## How Video Call Sextortion Works
- Victims receive a video call on WhatsApp or Instagram from an unknown number showing a woman undressing or explicit video clips.
- The scammers record the victim's screen and face, then immediately demand money threatening to send the recorded video to YouTube, Facebook, family members, or employer.
- They often send fake YouTube upload links or impersonate "Delhi Cyber Police" demanding deletion fees.

## Golden Rules for Victims
- DO NOT PANIC AND DO NOT PAY ANY MONEY. Once you pay, they will demand larger amounts.
- Block the scammer immediately on WhatsApp and social media.
- Set all your social media profiles (Instagram, Facebook) to Private immediately.
- Preserve evidence: take screenshots of the phone numbers, extortion chats, and payment demands before blocking.
- Call Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306 immediately for guidance.
- File complaint on cybercrime.gov.in.
