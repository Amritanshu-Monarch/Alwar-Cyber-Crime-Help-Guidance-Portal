# Helplines

Alwar Cyber Control Room: 8764874306 (Alwar Police Cyber Crime Desk). WhatsApp chat is also available on the same number.

National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930. Call immediately after any online financial fraud.

Police emergency: 112, 24x7 Rajasthan Police response.

Official national portal for filing cyber complaints online: cybercrime.gov.in

# Golden hour rule

Report online financial fraud immediately on 1930 or to the Alwar Control Room (8764874306). Reporting within about 2 hours gives the best chance of freezing the stolen money.

# Evidence

Do not delete any APK file, suspicious app, WhatsApp chat, screenshot, SMS or call log after a fraud. They are digital evidence for the police investigation. Keep bank statements and transaction receipts.

# Safety rules

Never share bank OTP, card PIN or internet banking password with anyone. Police and bank officials never ask for OTP, passwords or screen sharing apps.
Do not install screen sharing apps such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer or RustDesk at a caller's request.

# Fake calls and SMS

Suspicious calls, SMS or WhatsApp messages can be reported on the Chakshu service of Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in).

# Complaint letter

The portal has a Complaint Letter Generator that drafts a formal application to the Station House Officer (SHO) of the local police station. Attach bank statement or transaction receipt, and screenshots or call log evidence.

# Police stations in Alwar

Key stations listed on the portal: Kotwali Police Station (Alwar City), NEB Police Station (Alwar City), Aravali Vihar Thana (Alwar), Mahila Thana Alwar (women's desk).

# Tenant and servant verification

Verify tenants and household help before renting or hiring. Keep copies of their ID proof. Documents: Aadhaar or Voter ID copy, 2 passport size photographs, permanent address and phone number of previous employer. Services are available through the Rajasthan Police citizen services (police.rajasthan.gov.in).
