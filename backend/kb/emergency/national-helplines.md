# National Cyber Crime Helplines

## 1930 National Cyber Helpline
1930 is the national cyber financial fraud helpline operated under the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS).
- Operational 24x7 across India.
- If money was lost to online fraud, calling 1930 within the Golden Hour (first 2 hours) offers the highest chance of freezing funds in beneficiary accounts.
- When calling 1930, keep ready: victim bank name, debit account number, transaction ID (UTR/UPI reference), exact amount, date and time, and fraudster account/UPI details if known.
- The 1930 operator provides an acknowledgement number via SMS.

## 112 All-India Emergency
112 is the national emergency number for immediate physical safety, distress, or urgent police intervention.
- For physical threats, extortion in person, or urgent safety concerns, call 112 immediately.

## 1090 Women Helpline
1090 (and 1091 / 181) is the helpline dedicated to women facing harassment, stalking, or online abuse.
