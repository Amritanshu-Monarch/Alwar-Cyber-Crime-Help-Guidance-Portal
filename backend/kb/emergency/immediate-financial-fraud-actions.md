# Immediate Actions for Financial Fraud

## First 10 Minutes (Golden Hour)
When money is debited fraudulently via UPI, net banking, or debit/credit card:
1. Call 1930 immediately to freeze the money in the fraudster's intermediate wallet or bank account. Note down the acknowledgement number sent to your mobile.
2. Call your bank's customer care or use mobile banking to immediately block the debit card, credit card, UPI ID, or net banking access.
3. Note down the exact Transaction ID (UTR number / 12-digit UPI reference number) from your bank SMS or statement.
4. Call Alwar Cyber Control Room at 8764874306 for local coordination and assistance in Alwar district.

## Critical Evidence to Preserve
- Never delete debit SMS notifications, transaction history, or bank alert emails.
- Take full screenshots of transaction success screens showing date, time, and UTR.
- Preserve screenshots of payment QR codes, chat conversations with the fraudster, and phone call logs.
