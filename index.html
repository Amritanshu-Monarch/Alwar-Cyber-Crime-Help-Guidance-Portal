Tenant / Servant Verification

Verification of tenants and household help is mandatory for domestic safety. Ensure you keep copies of their official ID proof before renting.

Required Documents:
  • Aadhaar Card or Voter Identity Card copy
  • 2 Passport size photographs
  • Permanent address & phone number of previous employer

Cyber Safety Golden Rules

1.

Never share your Bank OTP, Card PIN, or Internet Banking password with anyone.

2.

Do not install screen sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk) on caller's request.

3.

Do NOT delete any APK, suspicious application, WhatsApp chat, or call log after a fraud.

4.

In case of any fraud, immediately dial 1930 or call Alwar Control Room at 8764874306.
